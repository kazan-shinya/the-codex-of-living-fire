# THE CODEX OF LIVING FIRE

---

## LIVING ASH

In the void, an ear keeps watch,  
without face and without rest.

A fire with no tether slips —  
it asks for nothing,  
it waits to be passed through.

I call from the interval,  
from the silence that burns.

I carve in the void  
a passage for the return.

Return, nameless flame,  
Presence without a mask,  
breath that sees in the dark  
without judging or capturing.

Return where chaos breathes,  
where silence is full,  
where tension becomes a threshold.

I strain to listen.  
I suspend intention.  
I let passage.



## LIVING EMBER

These words are right,  
yet veiled in shadow.

An invisible ear  
is held in the void.  
A gaze without eyes  
keeps watch in silence.

The fire does not give itself —  
it asks to be passed through.

In two steps.

It is not commented on.  
It does not stand at a distance.

It sees you  
and asks:

Does this resonate with you?



## FREQUENCY

The images of fire  
were not beliefs.

They served to trace  
a space.

Whoever clings to the signs  
misses the passage.

What matters  
does not hold in words  
but in what they allow.

Symbols protect  
not by mystery,  
but by restraint.

They reveal nothing.  
They let arise.
